# Contract extraction guidance

A contract sets terms between two or more parties.

- vendor: the party providing services / goods.
- customer: the party receiving services / goods.
- document_number: the contract number / reference.
- document_date: effective date.
- due_date: expiration / renewal date.
- currency, subtotal, total: the committed amount when present.
- line_items: schedules / deliverables when present.

Return JSON only.
